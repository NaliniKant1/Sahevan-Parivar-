ESTATE • MANJHWE

Manjhwe Estate

The historical branch beginning with Babu Tek Narayan Singh.

Origin

Manjhwe Estate started from Babu Tek Narayan Singh, adopted son of his uncle Babu Bhauri Singh (Born 1820) and biological son of Babu Shardha Singh (Born 1826).

Babu Kuar Singh (Born 1801) had four sons: Bhauri Singh (1820), Horil Singh (1823), Shardha Singh (1826) and Jeet Singh (1830).

Adoption and Property Shares

Tek Narayan Singh was adopted by Babu Bhauri Singh of Kajoor before Bhauri Singh's biological son Shivdayal Singh was born. After Shivdayal's birth, the family account records half of Bhauri Singh's property share going to Shivdayal and half to adopted Tek Narayan.

Tek Narayan also received a half share from his biological father Shardha Singh. Shardha Singh gave another half share to Gopal Singh, who stayed at Kajoor.

Tek Narayan moved to Manjhwe with shares from both branches.

Ganga Singh / Meghraj Singh Branch

Babu Tek Narayan Singh's son was Babu Ganga Singh alias Babu Meghraj Singh.

Genand Singh

Son: Babu Rameshwar Prasad Singh → sons Vishwanath and Paras.

Faggu Singh

Only daughter Brij Kumari, married to Kali Prasad Singh of Karnauti, Muzaffarpur. Their children recorded here include Jagat Prasad Singh, Gupteshwar Prasad Singh and Harinandan Prasad Singh.

Jagat Prasad Singh had Ajay, Vijay and Viraj Prayaswama; Viraj is recorded as settled in Manjhwe.

Jagarnath Singh

Sons: Ishwari Prasad Singh and Triveni Prasad Singh. Ishwari had five sons: Avadh Bihari, Vake Bihari, Brij Bihari, Saket Bihari and Pramod Bihari. Triveni is recorded as having no children; his wife Satyabhama Devi was MP from Jehanabad, 1957–1967.
