History of the Sahevan Family

A historical overview based on the family information supplied for this website.

01

The Zamindari

The Sahevan family of Village Kajoor, Block Mohra, District Gaya ji, Bihar, is remembered as a prominent zamindar family of the region. The historical holdings are described as extending from Maradi in Gaya district to Nazaddi in Nawada, with holdings in Jehanabad and Aurangabad.

The historical landholding is described as approximately 21,000 bigha.

02

Origin of the Dynasty

The dynasty chart begins with Babu Kuar Singh (Born 1801). According to the family history, the zamindari was purchased by his elder son Babu Bhauri Singh (Born 1820).

  • Babu Bhauri Singh — 1820
  • Babu Horil Singh — 1823
  • Babu Shardha Singh — 1826
  • Babu Jeet Singh — 1830
03

Expansion of Branches

The descendants of the four brothers developed different family branches, Zamindaris and Estates. The Manjhwe Estate branch is associated with Babu Tek Narayan Singh, who was adopted by Babu Bhauri Singh. Other are Zamindar Babu Harinandan Prasad Singh,Zamindar Babu Govind Prasad Singh, Zamindar Babu Kamla Prasad Singh , Zamindar Babu Chakradhar Prasad Singh Alias Jhulan Prasad Singh
