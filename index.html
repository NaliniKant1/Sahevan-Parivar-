History of the Sahevan Family

A historical overview of the Sahevan family of Kajoor, Mohra, Gaya ji, Bihar.

01

The Zamindari

The Sahevan family of Village Kajoor, Block Mohra, District Gaya ji, Bihar (805131), was a prominent zamindar family of the region.

The family's historical zamindari holdings extended from Maradi in Gaya district to Nazaddi in Nawada district, with holdings in several areas of Jehanabad including Kamta, Tabkla, Tuturkhi, Nanoak and Paleya, and in Aurangabad including Karpi/Kirpi and surrounding areas.

The historical landholding is described as approximately 21,000 bigha.

02

Origin of the Dynasty

The dynasty chart begins with Babu Kuar Singh (Born 1801).

According to the family history, the zamindari was purchased by his elder son Babu Bhauri Singh (Born 1820) from English Lueus Saheb.

The other sons of Babu Kuar Singh were:

  • Babu Horil Singh – Born 1823
  • Babu Shardha Singh – Born 1826
  • Babu Jeet Singh – Born 1830

The descendants of the four brothers subsequently developed different branches, estates and zamindaris.

03

Bhoodan Movement

The family donated land to the Bhoodan Movement associated with Vinoba Bhave, with the stated purpose of helping landless people and socially disadvantaged communities.

ESTATE • MANJHWE

Manjhwe Estate

The historical branch beginning with Babu Tek Narayan Singh.

The following lineage is presented from the family history supplied for this website.

Origin of Manjhwe Estate

Manjhwe Estate started from Babu Tek Narayan Singh, who was the adopted son of his uncle Babu Bhauri Singh (Born 1820) and the biological son of Babu Shardha Singh (Born 1826).

Babu Bhauri SinghBorn 1820

Babu Shardha SinghBorn 1826

Biological father of Tek Narayan Singh.

Adoption of Tek Narayan Singh

Babu Tek Narayan Singh was adopted by his uncle Babu Bhauri Singh of Village Kajoor when Babu Bhauri Singh's own son Babu Shivdayal Singh had not yet been born.

When Babu Shivdayal Singh was born, the property share of Babu Bhauri Singh was divided: half was given to his own son Babu Shivdayal Singh and half to his adopted son Babu Tek Narayan Singh.

Share from Biological Father

Babu Tek Narayan Singh also received a half share from his biological father, Babu Shardha Singh. Babu Shardha Singh also gave a half share to Babu Gopal Singh, who remained at Kajoor.

Settlement at Manjhwe

With the shares received from both branches, Babu Tek Narayan Singh shifted to Manjhwe. His branch subsequently developed into what is remembered within the family as the Manjhwe Estate.

ZAMINDAR • MANJHWE BRANCH

Zamindar Babu Harinandan Prasad Singh

A major branch of the Sahevan family associated with Manjhwe.

Lineage

Babu Harinandan Prasad Singh was the son of Sheetal Prasad Singh (married at Village Bampur near Chandi-Ramghat), grandson of Shivdayal Prasad Singh (Born 1846) (married at Village Shivnar near Mokama), and great-grandson of Babu Bhauri Singh (Born 1820), who was a son of Babu Kuar Singh (Born 1801).

Marriage and Brothers

Babu Harinandan Prasad Singh was married to Shiv Jyoti Devi of Jamuwan near Tajpur.

He had two brothers:

  1. Babu Banaras Prasad Singh, married to Suryamukhi Devi at Jamuwan near Tajpur.
  2. Babu Rajdev Narayan Singh, married to Shanti Devi of Balwa, Jehanabad.

Babu Banaras Prasad Singh Branch

Babu Banaras Prasad Singh had two sons: Bhagirath Prasad Singh and Birendra Prasad Singh.

Bhagirath Prasad Singh

Married at Dumrama near Nawada. He had one son, Mrityunjay Prasad Singh.

Mrityunjay Prasad Singh was married to Vaidehi Devi of Teaus, daughter of Mani Prasad Singh.

Children of Mrityunjay Prasad Singh

• Vikash Ranjan — son Gyan. Eminent software engineer; married to Aprajita of Auare near Lakhisarai.

• Prakash Ranjan — son Darshit. Business related to concrete; married to Priyanka of Janpura near Hisua.

Birendra Prasad Singh

Married to Panpati Devi of Bandhawa, Aurangabad. They had one daughter, Prabhavati Devi, married to Dr. Bimal Prasad Singh, former Principal of RDS College, Muzaffarpur, of Kebatgawan, Muzaffarpur.

Harinandan Prasad Singh → Madan Murari Prasad Singh

Babu Harinandan Prasad Singh had one son, Babu Madan Murari Prasad Singh, married to Hiramani Devi of Bandhwa, Aurangabad. They had five sons.

1. Arunjay Prasad Singh

Married to Pushpa Singh of Kafen, Muzaffarpur. Two sons: Parijat Dhawal and Sushnat Nirmal. Sushnat Nirmal is married to Meneka of Parepur near Bihta, Patna.

Arunjay Prasad Singh is described as an eminent engineer and Managing Director of several multinational companies.

Parijat Dhawal did B.Tech and M.Tech from IIT Kharagpur and MBA (Finance) from University of Michigan, USA; he is described as a finance-based entrepreneur.

2. Dhananjay Prasad Singh

Married to Nirmala Devi of Jehanabad. One son: Pankaj Kumar; Pankaj's son is Priyanshu.

3. Shashi Ranjan

Married to Madhurima Pandey of Sitalpur, Chhapra. Two sons: Nishikant and Rishikant. Nishikant is a specialised computer engineer.

4. Shashank Shekhar

Married to Nivedita Manju of Rampur Sinday near Sheikhpura. One son: Nalini Kant. Nalini Kant is a specialised electronics engineer.

5. Ravi Ranjan

Married to Kanchana of Narauli, Nawada. One son: Aarav Ritvik Singh. Ravi Ranjan is an eminent electronics engineer and currently Director at NXP Semiconductors.
