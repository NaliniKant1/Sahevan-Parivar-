Sahevan Family of Kajoor

The Sahevan family of Kajoor, Block Mohra, Gaya ji, Bihar — a family whose Zamindari Was From Maradi in Gaya District to Nazaddi in Nawada District ( As Present Day ) & holdings in Jahanabad ( Village - Kamta, Tabakla, Tuturkhi, Nanaok, Palyea etc.) and Aurangabad ( Village- Kirpi etc. ) District. Approximate Land Holding of 21000 Bigha
