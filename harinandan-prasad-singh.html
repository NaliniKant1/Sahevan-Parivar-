ZAMINDAR • MANJHWE BRANCH

Babu Harinandan Prasad Singh

A detailed family branch preserved from the supplied family history.

Lineage

Harinandan Prasad Singh was the son of Sheetal Prasad Singh, grandson of Shivdayal Prasad Singh (Born 1846), and great-grandson of Babu Bhauri Singh (Born 1820), son of Babu Kuar Singh (Born 1801).

Sheetal Prasad Singh was married at Bampur near Chandi-Ramghat; Shivdayal Prasad Singh was married at Shivnar near Mokama.

Marriage and Brothers

Harinandan Prasad Singh married Shiv Jyoti Devi of Jamuwan near Tajpur.

  • Elder brother Babu Banaras Prasad Singh — married Suryamukhi Devi at Jamuwan near Tajpur.
  • Younger brother Babu Rajdev Narayan Singh — married Shanti Devi of Balwa, Jehanabad.

Banaras Prasad Singh Branch

Banaras Prasad Singh had Bhagirath Prasad Singh and Birendra Prasad Singh.

Bhagirath Prasad Singh

Son: Mrityunjay Prasad Singh, married Vaidehi Devi of Teaus, daughter of Mani Prasad Singh.

Children recorded: Vikash Ranjan (son Gyan) and Prakash Ranjan (son Darshit). Vikash is described as an eminent software engineer; Prakash is associated with concrete-industry business.

Birendra Prasad Singh

Married Panpati Devi of Bandhawa, Aurangabad. Daughter Prabhavati Devi married Dr. Bimal Prasad Singh, former Principal of RDS College, Muzaffarpur, of Kebatgawan, Muzaffarpur.

Harinandan → Madan Murari Prasad Singh

Harinandan had one son, Madan Murari Prasad Singh, married Hiramani Devi of Bandhwa, Aurangabad. Their five sons are recorded below.

1. Arunjay Prasad Singh

Married Pushpa Singh of Kafen, Muzaffarpur. Sons Parijat Dhawal and Sushnat Nirmal. Sushnat married Meneka of Parepur near Bihta, Patna.

Arunjay is described as an eminent engineer and MD of multinational companies. Parijat completed B.Tech/M.Tech at IIT Kharagpur and MBA Finance at University of Michigan and is described as a finance entrepreneur.

2. Dhananjay Prasad Singh

Married Nirmala Devi of Jehanabad. Son Pankaj Kumar; grandson Priyanshu.

3. Shashi Ranjan

Married Madhurima Pandey of Sitalpur, Chhapra. Sons Nishikant and Rishikant. Nishikant is a specialised computer engineer.

4. Shashank Shekhar

Married Nivedita Manju of Rampur Sinday near Sheikhpura. Son Nalini Kant, a specialised electronics engineer.

5. Ravi Ranjan

Married Kanchana of Narauli, Nawada. Son Aarav Ritvik Singh. Ravi is described as an eminent electronics engineer and currently Director at NXP Semiconductors.
